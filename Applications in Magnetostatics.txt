Applications in Magnetostatics
As a branch of electromagnetics that focuses on static magnetic fields, magnetostatics finds numerous important applications in engineering and science. Below are some of the main areas where the study of magnetostatics is highly relevant. It is worth noting that in some of these applications, magnetostatics may not cover all of the electromagnetics design requirements.

DC Machines (Direct Current Machines)
Electromagnetic Brakes and Clutches
Linear and rotational actuators
Loudspeakers
Magnetic bearings
Magnetic Levitation Devices
Magnetic Recording
MEMS
Motors and Generators
MRI
Permanent Magnet Motors
Relays
Solenoids
Sensors
