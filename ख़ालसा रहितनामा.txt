
Khalsa Rehatnama,
ਖ਼ਾਲਸਾ ਰਹਿਤ ਨਾਮਾ,
ख़ालसा रहित नामा


ख़ालसा रहितनामा॥

The Khalsa way of life (code of conduct).

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#1)

ੴ वाहिगुरू जी की फतह ॥

There is one Lord Creator; victory be to the Lord.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#2)

श्री मुखवाक पातशाही १०॥

Verbal Declaration of the Tenth Sovereign.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#3)

सोरठा॥

Soratha.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#4)

प्रथम रहित यह जान खंडे की पाहुल छके॥

Know that the first injunction of the Rehit is to undertake the Amrit of the two-edged sword.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#5)

सोई सिंघ प्रधान अवर न पाहुल जो लए॥१॥

Such a Singh is superior who takes Amrit of no other kind.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#6)

पाँच सिंघ अंमृत जो देवैं॥

The Amrit which the five Singhs give;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#7)

ताँ को सिर धर छक पुन लेवै॥२॥

He should bow his head and receive it.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#8)

पुन मिल पाँचहु रहित जो भाखहिं॥

Then the Rehit which the five Singhs deliver;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#9)

ताँ को मन मैं दृड़ कर राखहि॥३॥

He should fix it within his heart.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#10)

कुड़ी मार आदिक हैं जेते॥

Those who kill their daughters and commit other such evil deeds;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#11)

मन ते दूर तिआगे तेते॥४॥

Keep well away from them, renounce them.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#12)

बाणी माहि नेहु नित कारनो॥

Every day show your love for the Guru's Bani.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#13)

चुगली निंदा अर परहरनो॥५॥

Renounce backbiting slandering, and the property of other's.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#14)

प्रथम रहत एही है कही॥

The first commands of the Rehit are those;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#15)

पाहुल मैं जो सिंघन कही॥६॥

Which the Singhs have prescribed to you in the Pahul ceremony.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#16)

और जु रहत कही है नान्हा॥

The other injunctions of the Rehit;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#17)

सो तुम आगे करहु भखानाँ॥७॥

I say before you so you may explain to other Singhs.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#18)

वाहगुरू नित बचन उचारे॥

Every day utter Waheguru.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#19)

वाहगुरू को हिरदै धारे॥८॥

Imbue the mantra of Waheguru in your heart.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#20)

आगे आवत सिंघ जो पावै॥

When a Singh comes across you;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#21)

वाहगुरू की फतहि बुलावै॥९॥

Greet him with Waheguru Ji Ka Khalsa Waheguru Ji Ki Fateh.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#22)

प्रातहि उठ इशनानहि करै॥

Arise at the last quarter of night and take a bath.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#23)

पुन मुख ते जपु जापु उचरै॥१०॥

Then recite your morning prayers such as JapJi and Jaap.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#24)

यथाशकति किछ देवहि दान॥

Give to charity as your resources permit;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#25)

सोई सिंघ है परम सुजान॥११॥

Such a Singh is supremely Wise.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#26)

नाम दान प्राते इसनाना॥

Naam, charity and bathing at morning hours;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#27)

जिन गुर कृपा तिनहु इह जाना॥१२॥

Are known and practiced by those whom the Guru has been gracious to.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#28)

दस नख जो कार कमावै॥

Profit which you have made with the ten nails of your hands (honest earnings);

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#29)

ताँ कर जो धन घर मै आवै॥१३॥

Sustain your household only with such an income.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#30)

तिहते गुर दसवंध जु देई॥

The Singh who donates a tenth of his income to the Guru;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#31)

सिंघ सु जस बहु जग महि लेई॥१४॥

shall be greatly honored in this world.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#32)

पर बेटी को बेटी जानै॥

Consider another's daughter as your own daughter;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#33)

पर इशत्री को मात बखानै॥१५॥

Consider another's wife as your own mother.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#34)

आपनि इशत्री सों रति होई॥

One who remains loyal to his own wife;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#35)

रहतवंत सिंघ है सोई॥१६॥

Is a Rehitvant (one who is dedicated to the Rehit) Singh.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#36)

काम क्रोध मान मद लोभा॥

Lust, anger, pride, intoxication, and greed;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#37)

इह वैरी कर है मन छोभा॥१७॥

These are the enemies which afflict the heart.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#38)

इन को हठ कर जीवत करै॥

One who resists them begins to truly live life.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#39)

दया धरम तप सो मन धरै॥१८॥

Keep within your heart Compassion, righteousness, and austerity.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#40)

कछ कृपान न कबहूँ तिआगै॥

Never be separated from your Kachera and Kirpan.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#41)

सनमुख लरै न रण ते भागै॥१९॥

Fight the enemy directly, never turn your back and flee from the battlefield.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#42)

दे अरदास निवावै सीस॥

When reciting Ardas bow your head.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#43)

मन मैं धयावै गुर जगदीस॥२०॥

Meditate upon the supreme Guru within your heart.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#44)

जहा खालसा बैठा पावै॥

Wherever you happen to find a Khalsa;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#45)

वाहिगुरू जी की फते बुलावै॥२१॥

Greet him with Waheguru Ji Ki Fateh.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#46)

प्रेम सहत जो दरशन कर है॥

One who comes for Darshan with love;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#47)

ते पुन भव सागर नहि पर है॥२२॥

Will not have to cross over the World-ocean again.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#48)

तुरक देख नहि सीस निवावै॥

One who does not bow before a Turk (Mughal).

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#49)

रहितवंत सो सिंघ कहावै॥२३॥

Such a Singh is called Rehatvant (one obedient to the Rehat).

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#50)

नौकर तुरकन का नहिं होइं॥

Do not become a servant of the Turks (ruling Mughals).

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#51)

तुरक सलाम करे नहि कोइ॥२४॥

No one shall greet with the Salaam (salutations) of the Turks.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#52)

तुरकन पर बिश्वास न करीए॥

Do not trust the Turks.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#53)

कर मैत्री नहि बिवहरीए॥२५॥

Have no friendly dealings with them.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#54)

नौकर होइ खालसे केरा॥

Be the servant of the Khalsa solely;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#55)

जिन मिलिआँ सुख होइ घनेरा॥२६॥

Meeting whom one finds much bliss.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#56)

जिन दरशन कर पाप मिटाँही॥

Having Darshan of whom obliterates sins.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#57)

मन प्रसंन कुछ चिंता नाही॥२७॥

With content hearts they know no worries.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#58)

गुरू सरूप खालसा हईए॥

The very image of the Guru is the Khalsa;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#59)

जिन की टहिल परमसुख लहीए॥२८॥

From whose service celestial peace is obtained.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#60)

दोहरा॥

Dohra.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#61)

नौकर होइ न सकई करै सु आन उपाइ॥

If one cannot render service, he should seek work elsewhere.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#62)

जिह बिधि रहत न नास होइ सोई टहिल कमाइ॥२९॥

One should practice a profession which does not involve any violation of the Rehat.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#63)

खेती वणज व सिलप बणावै॥

Engage in Farming, trade, handicrafts;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#64)

और टहिल जो मन मो भावै॥३०॥

or any other profession which pleases your heart.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#65)

दृड़्ह हुई सोइ कार कमावै॥

Earn through hard and honest work.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#66)

चौरी डाके कबहि न जावै॥३१॥

Never steal or loot.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#67)

बेसया बिखया जूआ तजै॥

Avoid prostitutes, intoxicants, and gambling.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#68)

मछली मास न कबहूँ भजै॥३२॥

Fish and animal's flesh, never go after these.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#69)

घर मो साध सिंघ जो आई॥

Saintly Singhs who may come to your home;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#70)

तिन की बहु बिधि सेव कराई॥३३॥

Serve them in many ways

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#71)

चरन धोइ प्रसाद जिवावै॥

Wash their feat and offer food.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#72)

हरि के भाव सभन मो लिआवै॥३४॥

Treat and serve every person with the love of God.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#73)

दोहरा॥

Dohra.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#74)

पाठ ग्रंथ को होइ जहिं जहिं हरि कथा बखान॥

Wherever the prayers and exegesis of the Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji are being recited;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#75)

तह कछु अवर न बोलई सुनीए लाइ धिआन॥३५॥

Do not speak there and listen with concentration.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#76)

चौपई॥

Chaupai.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#77)

कुट्ठा हुक्का चरस तमाकू॥

Kutha, Hookah, Cannabis, Tobacco;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#78)

गाँजा टोपी ताड़ी खाकू॥३६॥

Ganja (hemp), tobacco toddy, and other such ruinous substances.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#79)

इन की ओर न कबहू देखै॥

One who never even looks upon these;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#80)

रहतवंत सो सिंघ विसेखै॥३७॥

Consider him my Rehatvant Singh.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#81)

शसत्रहीन कबहू न होई॥

One who is never without weapons;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#82)

रहितवंत ख़ालसा है सोई॥३८॥

Know him to be my Rehatvant Khalsa.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#83)

संनिआसी बैरागी जोवै॥

Beware of ascetics Sanyasis and Bairaagis;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#84)

और उदासी जोगी तेवै॥३९॥

Avoid Udasis and Yogis.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#85)

जंगम बामी अवर जि कोई॥

Shaivites and Bami (Tantric priests),

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#86)

ताँ का जूठा कबी न लेई॥४०॥

Never eat the food defiled by them.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#87)

परमारथ गुरू एको कहिओ॥

The supreme truth is the one which the Guru has uttered;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#88)

जिन के घर बस गुन कछु लहिओ॥४१॥

From who's house virtues are obtained.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#89)

ठाकुर जान सकल सो सेवै॥

Serve the Gurus (Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji and Sri Guru Khalsa Panth) as are your lords.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#90)

भाव सहित कुछ कर ते देवै॥४२॥

Act with love and give charity in their name.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#91)

गुरमुखी अक्खर जो हैं भाई॥

The letters of the Gurmukhi alphabet which the Guru has created;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#92)

सिंघ सिंघ ते सीखहि जाई॥४३॥

A Singh should learn them from another Singh.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#93)

और जु विदया जहिं तहिं होई॥

Other knowledge and sciences that exist from far and wide;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#94)

अवरन ते भी लेवहु सोई॥४४॥

Strive to learn them from others.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#95)

दोहरा॥

Dohra.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#96)

प्रातह उठ इसनान करि पड़हि जापु जपु दोइ॥

Arise at the last quarter of night, bathe and then start the morning prayers by reading both Japji and Jaap,

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#97)

सोदर की चौंकी करे आलस करै न कोइ॥४५॥

Recite the Sodar at evening, do not be lazy about this.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#98)

पहरि रात बीत है जबही॥

When the first quarter of night arrives;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#99)

सोहिला पाठ करै सो तबही॥४६॥

Recite the Sohila then.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#100)

दुहूँ ग्रंथ मैं बाणी जोई॥

The Bani enshrined in both Granths;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#101)

चुन चुन कंठ करे नित सोई॥४७॥

Select portions from each and commit them to memory.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#102)

दसमी आदि गुरू दिन जेते॥

Days associated with the Gurus, from the tenth to the first;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#103)

पुरब समान कहे हैं तेते॥४८॥

Celebrate them as festivals and holy days.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#104)

तिन मो कछु प्रसादि बणावै॥

Prepare food on these occasions.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#105)

कर कड़ाह खालसे खवावै॥४९॥

Cook the food in an iron bowl and offer it to the Khalsa.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#106)

यथाशकति अरदासि भि देवै॥

Give as much as you can afford when offering Ardas.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#107)

खाली हाथ ना कबहूँ सेवै॥५०॥

Never come with an empty hand.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#108)

आप सिंघ जो राजा होई॥

A Singh who is a ruler;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#109)

निरधन सिंघन पालै सोई॥५१॥

He should care for the poorer Singhs.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#110)

निरधन खालसे नेहु लगावै॥

Show love for the poorer Khalsas.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#111)

धनबाज़ी दे प्रीति बढावै॥५२॥

Increase love for them by giving them horses, and benefactions.

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#112)

परदेसी सिंघन जब देखै॥

When one finds Singhs who come from afar;

Bhai Nand Lal ji and Guru Gobind Singh ji / / / Khalsa Rehatnama (#113)

इन की सेवा करै विसेखै॥५३॥

He must serve them whole-heartedly.
