Magnet Tests

1. visual test

2. Steel ball test -- for acertaining whether all coils of a quadrupole for example, have same length (5 bar continous air pressure, 3 mm dia steel balls)

3. water pressure test : close one end of the coil. Apply 20 bar pressure. It will drop to 18 bar or so. Then observe that it does not change much within 30 minutes.

4. Thermal switch test : Power the magnet at full current. stop water flow. See if the circuit trips due to temp rise. record the closing and opening temp of the switch.

5. Insulation between yoke and coil : (1) 500 V DC for 1 minute (2) 5 kV DC for 1 minute. Measure with cooling water. 

6. Capacitive discharge test : first 1,25 kV and then 5 kV . 

7. Inductance test : at 1 Hz, 20 Hz, 100 Hz, and 1 kHz frequencies.

8. Resistance of coils : with 4 wire method, using about 1A current.

9. Pulsed current test

10. Magnetic test : (1) Field harmonic test  (2) Integral field test  (B vs I , or G vs I test) (3)
